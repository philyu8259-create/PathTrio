Privacy Policy

PathTrio Privacy Policy

PathTrio is designed as a local-first workout tracker. We keep data collection limited to the features you choose to use.

Data We Process

  • Workout records such as activity type, start time, duration, distance, pace, speed, calories, and route points.
  • Location data while an active workout is being recorded.
  • Motion and fitness activity signals when Smart Assist is enabled.
  • Health data only when you choose to enable Health sync.
  • Purchase entitlement information from Apple to unlock Pro features.

Local Storage

Workout records are stored locally on your device by default. PathTrio does not operate its own account system or cloud database for your workouts.

Location Use

Location is used to calculate distance, speed, and GPS routes during an active workout. Background location is used only when needed to continue a workout after the screen locks.

HealthKit

If you enable Health sync, PathTrio may write eligible walking, running, or cycling workouts to Apple Health. Health data is handled through Apple's HealthKit permission system.

Apple Watch

When Apple Watch support is available and enabled, PathTrio may send workout status and summary information between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch.

Data Sharing

PathTrio does not sell your personal data. Exported CSV or GPX files are shared only when you choose to export or share them.

Deleting Data

You can delete workouts in the app or remove the app from your device to delete local PathTrio data stored by the app.
